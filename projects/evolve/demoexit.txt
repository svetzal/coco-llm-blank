# The quick demo build: defines QUICKDEMO, for the check only. The demo starts
# after 2 scans on the title without a key, and each wait of the demo is 1 scan.
# Keys purpose. Line 1 is the startup sample; line N is held at scan N.
# U/D/L/R = arrows, F = Space, - = none.
-    startup sample
-    the title: 1 scan without a key
-    2 scans: the demo starts
-    the first key of the demo lights
L    an arrow ends the demo: the title
-    1 scan without a key
-    2 scans: the demo starts again
U    an arrow ends the demo: the title
U    a held key on the title starts the count again
-    1 scan without a key
-    2 scans: the demo starts again
F    Space in the demo starts a new game
-    release
L    a move of the new game
