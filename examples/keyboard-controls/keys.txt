# Keys position actions purpose. Line 1 is the startup sample; lines 2-17
# are updates 1-16. L/R = left/right arrow, F = Space, M = M, - = none.
# Position and actions are written by hand from the policy in README.md.
F    2 0  startup sample seeds the history
F    2 0  startup-held Space is not a press
-    2 0  release
RF   3 1  movement and action together
RFM  4 1  M shares the left column on another row: not left
RF   4 1  blocked at the right edge, Space held
R    4 1  release Space while blocked
RF   4 2  press Space again while blocked
RF   4 2  a held Space does not repeat
LF   3 2  move back with Space held
L    2 2  release Space while moving
LF   1 3  press Space again while moving
LRF  1 3  opposite arrows cancel
-    1 3  release all
L    0 3  left
L    0 3  blocked at the left edge
R    1 3  move back from the left edge
